# OEWG-15 Item 1 — Cover Email Draft

**To:** Secretariat of the Basel Convention — brs@un.org
**Subject:** OEWG-15/3 pilot testing results — technical guidelines on e-waste and used EEE

Dear Secretariat,

Please find attached DexMetal LLC's pilot-testing results concerning the technical guidelines on transboundary movements of electrical and electronic waste and used electrical and electronic equipment, in particular regarding the distinction between waste and non-waste under the Basel Convention (UNEP/CHW.16/INF/10/Rev.1).

The pilot focused on converting the guideline's waste/non-waste distinction and evidentiary conditions into a deterministic decision workflow, then testing that workflow against ten realistic scenarios. The report records the method, test cases, results, ambiguities identified, and corrections made where legacy classification logic conflicted with the current e-waste entries.

The submission is intended as practical pilot feedback for consideration by the expert working group pursuant to OEWG-15/3. It is not presented as a competent-authority determination, and national law and competent-authority decisions remain controlling.
